PestFlow brand system · Version 1.0

Operational clarity for pest control companies.

A complete identity system for a Malaysian pest-control operating platform: calm under pressure, specific to the field, and precise enough for compliance and finance.

01 · Brand strategy

The system behind every service visit.

Purpose. Help pest control companies connect commercial work, field execution, service evidence, customer records and billing in one operating system.

Position. PestFlow is not generic field-service software. It is an operational record built around recurring pest-control service, field verification, deployed assets, service reports, SST and MyInvois workflows.

Promise. Make the next decision and the underlying record clear to each role.

Personality. Precise, calm, field-aware, local, direct and quietly confident.

02 · Logo system

Compact, legible, operational.

The icon and wordmark work together by default. Use the icon alone only where space is constrained or the brand is already established.

Clear space

Keep at least one quarter of the icon width around every side. Do not place text, rules or imagery inside this area.

Minimum size

Digital lockup: 120px wide minimum.

Icon: 24px minimum; 30–36px preferred in navigation.

Never stretch, rotate, recolor, add glow, crop the mark, or place it on visually noisy detail.

05 · Layout & spacing

One message per viewport.

Product UI carries the visual weight. Copy explains the next decision. White space separates operating stages.

24324880120

Container: 1000px core, 1200px wide

Gutter: 24px desktop/tablet, 16px mobile

Grid gap: 32px product cards, 18px utilities

Section rhythm: 120px desktop, 80px mobile

Radius: 24px hero cards, 20px panels, 12–16px controls

06 · Motion

Motion explains state and handoff.

Animation should make workflow easier to understand—not decorate empty space.

01

Route motion

Use scroll-linked crossfades to show office → field → customer → finance state changes.

02

Field atmosphere

Animated gradient columns move 5–8 seconds with varied phase. Keep copy on a stable contrast overlay.

03

Interaction

Buttons use 160–220ms transform or color feedback. Avoid large bounce, cursor tricks and continuous blur.

04

Reduced motion

Freeze decorative motion, remove sticky storytelling and show every state in normal flow.

08 · Imagery & product evidence

Show the system doing pest-control work.

Prefer real interface evidence over stock photography and abstract feature icons.

Use route lines, site grids, QR cells, deployed-asset labels, service timestamps and chemical/report notation. Avoid cartoon insects, shields, spray icons and generic “AI” sparkles.

09 · Voice & language

Direct enough for the office. Clear enough for the field.

Do“Check in at the assigned site.”“Report could not sync. Try again when signal returns.”“3 recurring visits need assignment.”
Avoid“Seamlessly elevate your field operations.”“Oops! Something went wrong.”“Unlock game-changing productivity.”
EnglishPlain, specific, active voice. Use Malaysian operational terms where established.
Bahasa MelayuNatural business Malay, not word-for-word machine translation. Keep product names and regulated terms consistent.

10 · Accessibility

Clarity is part of the brand.

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Automated checks support—but do not replace—manual keyboard, contrast and assistive-technology testing.

Every PestFlow touchpoint should feel operationally clear.

When in doubt, choose the more specific message, the real product screen and the quieter interaction.
