Why PestFlow exists

One job should not become five versions of the truth.

Pest control teams coordinate commercial promises, recurring schedules, technician visits, service evidence, customer records and billing. When every team rebuilds that information in a different tool, the operation becomes harder to see and harder to improve.

PestFlow’s stated purpose is to keep those handoffs connected in one operating record, shaped around Malaysian field teams and back-office work.
